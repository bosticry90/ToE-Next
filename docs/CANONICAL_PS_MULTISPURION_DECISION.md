## Decision that must not be collapsed

Two questions are independent:

1. **EFT control:** Can a declared small-parameter family give a uniformly invertible upper-heavy block, bounded heavy–light mixing and a truncation error acceptable at `epsilon=sigma/omega=0.1`?
2. **Origin of smallness:** Are the needed small parameter combinations symmetry-protected, radiatively stable only under additional premises, or unprotected tunings?

An unprotected tuning can still define a mathematically controlled EFT. It increases the benchmark's assumption/naturalness burden; it is **not** by itself `PS_INTERVAL_EFT_POWER_COUNTING_FAIL` or a proof that PS running is impossible. Conversely, an enlarged symmetry of a quadratic matrix alone does not protect a parameter unless it is a symmetry of the relevant **interacting** action. The frozen [`PARENT_ACTION_V1`](../calculations/canonical_so10_scalar_reconstruction/PARENT_ACTION_V1.md) completely defines the scalar potential, kinetic conventions and PQ charges, but the [canonical definition record](../calculations/canonical_so10_scalar_reconstruction/RECORD.md) explicitly leaves parent Yukawa contraction/normalization unfrozen. A scalar-sector enhanced-symmetry result must not be promoted to a full-theory naturalness claim without that check.

## Direct physical-vacuum fallback: scope, not admission

If no PS-covariant interval truncation can be controlled to a declared accuracy—or if the project deliberately rejects the required unprotected tuning burden—prepare a *separate* `Spin(10) -> SM` gauge-matching gate around the **actual stationary** positive-Higgs vacuum. This does not erase the exact PS subgroup or the two vector mass bands. It avoids assuming a renormalizable PS running interval that has not been derived.

The smallest defensible fallback is a **one-step, fixed-order, SM-background-field hard match** below the lightest integrated heavy state. Keep the tuned Higgs and PQ Goldstone light and explicitly disposition any SM-neutral right-handed neutrinos; do not silently integrate an unfitted singlet sector into an otherwise “complete” low EFT. The parent-side coupling is the renormalized `g_10` in one frozen scheme. Match the three unbroken-SM `F_a²` coefficients from the physical scalar and vector mass **operators**, with all 33 massive vectors, their Goldstones/ghosts, and the physical heavy scalars counted once. Use basis-invariant spectral/matrix functions so mixed states require no artificial PS labels. Derive finite and logarithmic terms in one UV/EFT gauge-fixing and subtraction convention. Require the direct Spin(10)-to-SM beta-jump and matching-scale derivative checks, known degenerate limits, and independent replay.

This route's advantage is that the physical mass operators commute with the unbroken **SM** generators, unlike the upper-boundary PS-generator problem. Its cost is that a one-step match contains the full upper/lower mass-ratio logarithms and does **not** resum them through ordinary renormalizable PS running. Quantify the largest logarithms and fixed-order uncertainty before interpreting a gauge-coupling intersection. Multiple SM-covariant decoupling steps are a possible later extension, but retaining light massive vectors in an intermediate theory requires an explicit BRST-consistent field/operator construction; it is **not** obtained by simply treating them as ordinary SM matter vectors. No previous partial lower-vector finite coefficient may be transplanted into the direct formula without verifying the common scheme and hard subtraction.

Fallback admission is therefore conditional on a separate calculation record freezing: low-energy field content, matching scale/window, gauge fixing, operator basis, heavy/light subtraction, treatment of large logs, physical VEV/gauge-coupling mass relations, and an uncertainty/stop rule. A direct one-loop match could test this benchmark against measured SM gauge couplings without a PS interval, but it would **not** establish global BFB, flavor/neutrino viability, proton stability, or a Theory of Everything. `BFB_UNRESOLVED` remains visible on either route.
