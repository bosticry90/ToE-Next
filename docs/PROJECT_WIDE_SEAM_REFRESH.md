## Selection question and rubric

Which unresolved relation now has a sufficiently specified source, established destination, explicit recovery map, cheap candidate-specific falsifier, credible observable path, and bounded authority to earn a first calculation? A *relation class* is only a search domain; an active [seam](OPEN_SEAMS.md) must identify a concrete missing arrow. An established theory may be the source. Novelty is not required.

The first six criteria are **admission gates**, not a weighted score:

1. **Specified source:** an attributable construction with dynamics, relevant state or background, field content, parameters, and independent assumptions frozen for the proposed arrow.
2. **Established destination:** a named lower-level description and domain, with variables, conventions, precision and provenance adequate to test recovery.
3. **Explicit missing map:** a particular reduction, matching, emergence or compatibility relation, including regime, approximation order and error terms; a broad topic does not qualify.
4. **Cheap falsifier:** a candidate-specific contradiction, forbidden limit, symmetry/consistency failure or discriminating prediction that can be checked before a large program. Failure must have a narrow interpretation.
5. **Observable path:** a plausible route from the mapped result to an operational quantity or independently testable reference, with known uncertainty and existing infrastructure identified.
6. **Bounded decision:** predeclared pass/fail/blocked conditions and an authority ceiling; success must not be misreported as validation of a whole framework.

Among relations passing those gates, compare **ToE leverage, potential genuine assumption compression, computational/storage cost, mature external machinery, and independent verification**. A purported compression must count new boundary conditions, states, couplings, fit choices and approximations. It is not inferred from elegant notation or from reproducing a known result. No favorable tie-breaker can rescue an undefined source or arrow. This is the [charter](../CHARTER.md) and original [first-selection](SEAM_SELECTION.md) rule applied to the present evidence, not a new policy.

## Decision and reopening rule

No row passes all admission gates. Therefore the answer to **“what should ToE-Next derive next?”** is presently `NONE_CURRENTLY_ADMISSIBLE`, not a forced pivot. This is a selection result, not a pause of ToE research or a claim that no relevant calculation can ever be found. The two Babu–Khan passes, their downstream flavor block, the GR EFT and joint EFT controls, spin-2 conditional relations, and quasiclassical reference ledger retain their prior authority ceilings.

Reopen selection when **one new attributable source-specific input** supplies enough information to test an arrow: for example, a validated same-model flavor point, a gravity source with a bounded matter-coupled limit, a cosmological mechanism with its perturbation/observable map, or a microscopic statistical model with a genuinely unresolved thermodynamic recovery. First test that input against the six gates. Do not rerun every literature survey, install a framework, begin a fit, or create a calculation directory merely because a broad class remains important.
