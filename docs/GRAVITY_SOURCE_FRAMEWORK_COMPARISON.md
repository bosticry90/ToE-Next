This is a bounded comparison of source *frameworks*, not a proof that no viable gravity theory or model-specific seam exists. The two passed Babu–Khan baryon-number-violation recovery steps remain valid at their stated ceilings; neither an unfinished flavor fit nor the rejected unchanged 2024 numerical point is promoted into a gravity premise. The local-custody [quadratic-gravity receipt](LEGACY_HANDOFF.md#quadratic-gravity-bounded-disposition) remains a reference and adversarial control, not an adopted theory.

## Selection standard

A source earns a seam only if one attributable construction fixes its dynamics, state or background, matter coupling, and regime well enough to state a controlled GR-recovery claim and a cheap *candidate-specific* falsifier, with a credible path to a measured quantity. Quantum consistency and the distinct quantum-to-quasiclassical-to-classical recovery obligations must be assessed at the authority claimed; a first bounded seam need not solve all of them. The comparison also asks whether independent assumptions are removed or moved into a vacuum, truncation, regulator, coupling, boundary condition, or matter sector. A cheap falsifier must test this specified construction, not a whole research tradition by association.

The rows are not a ranking by elegance. Some have stronger candidate UV motivation; others have cheaper observables. None presently combines both in one adequately frozen recovery-and-test chain. The available experimental controls, including [Cassini](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature01997) and the [GW170817/GRB 170817A propagation comparison](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.05834), become candidate falsifiers only after a source predicts their quantities in a controlled domain.

## Authority and reopening condition

`NONE` means no source framework is admitted **from this comparison** and no gravity calculation or seam is activated. It does not reject perturbative strings, asymptotic safety, spin foams, CDT, scalar-tensor theories, or all quadratic-gravity variants. It also does not make GR EFT a UV completion. Software availability is not the limiting factor, so no package, simulation, model tree, or dataset is added.

Reopen only for one specified source that supplies: (1) mathematical dynamics and a bounded quantum-consistency claim; (2) a precise matter-coupled GR-recovery target; (3) an explicit quasiclassical obligation and any current gap; (4) a parameter/assumption ledger; and (5) a cheap candidate-specific falsifier plus a credible operational-observable path. A first calculation may test just one arrow with a predeclared failure rule and authority ceiling. It should target the weakest arrow, not run an end-to-end phenomenology pipeline by default.
