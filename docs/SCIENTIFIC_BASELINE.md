# Scientific Baseline

## Purpose

This document states the broad established structures and empirical obligations that ToE-Next must respect or recover. It is deliberately conservative. It is not a catalogue of all physics, an endorsement of one ultraviolet completion, or a declaration that successful effective descriptions identify fundamental mechanisms.

## Baseline commitments

### Standard Model and quantum field theory

The tested gauge, field, symmetry, quantum, and particle-content structure of the Standard Model is a required low-energy recovery target. Quantum field theory supplies the established framework for relativistic quantum fields and interactions in its demonstrated domains.

This commitment does not assert that the Standard Model is complete, explain its parameter pattern, choose a grand-unified completion, or settle quantum gravity.

### General relativity

General relativity is the gravitational baseline in regimes where it has been tested and in the low-energy classical domain it successfully describes. A broader construction must recover its established predictions to the required accuracy.

This does not assert that general relativity is the final ultraviolet theory of gravity or that its classical formulation resolves every quantum, singularity, or cosmological question.

### Effective field theory

Effective field theory is an accepted method for organizing scale-separated physics, operators, symmetries, matching, and controlled corrections. A finite-domain success must be reported with its cutoff, expansion, assumptions, and error control; it is not automatically a fundamental completion.

### Thermodynamics and statistical mechanics

Thermodynamic laws, statistical consistency, entropy accounting, stability, and the established relation between microscopic and macroscopic descriptions are recovery obligations. Proposed fundamental structures must not evade these constraints through relabeling.

### Cosmology

Cosmological observations—including expansion history, large-scale structure, gravitational phenomena, radiation backgrounds, and abundance constraints—must be reproduced within their empirical uncertainties. Successful effective cosmological models are valid comparators in their tested domains.

Observed phenomena, effective descriptions, and underlying mechanisms must remain distinct. Successful phenomenology does not establish the identity of dark matter, the mechanism of dark energy, a particular inflationary mechanism, initial conditions, or a final quantum-cosmological theory.

## Surviving legacy content under established names

The historical project contains useful known-physics components. When relevant, they are carried forward under their established identities:

| Established identity | Bounded role |
|---|---|
| Cubic defocusing nonlinear Schrödinger / Gross–Pitaevskii equation | Known effective physics comparator |
| Madelung representation | Known hydrodynamic representation |
| Acoustic metric | Analogue-gravity diagnostic |
| Pillars and seams | Integration methodology |

These are not active “CCFT modules.” Their presence does not revive CCFT/UEFM as a ToE candidate. The historical disposition is recorded in [`LEGACY_HANDOFF.md`](LEGACY_HANDOFF.md).

## Deliberately unsettled

This baseline does not choose among proposed explanations of dark matter, dark energy, inflation, quantum measurement, baryogenesis, neutrino mass origin, the hierarchy structure, quantum gravity, or initial conditions. It also does not turn those broad questions into active seams.

An explanation becomes part of the project only through a precise claim, adequate provenance, explicit assumptions, a recovery obligation, and evidence appropriate to its authority ceiling.

A construction that modifies quantum or gravitational foundations must recover observed quasiclassical behavior in a specified regime, including appropriate interference suppression, stable coarse-grained variables, and effective classical dynamics; this requirement selects no particular interpretation of quantum mechanics.

## Recovery standard

“Recovering” established physics means more than reproducing suggestive notation. The broader construction must identify the domain, variables, approximations, parameter map, observables, limiting procedure, and error or control conditions under which the accepted lower-level description follows.
