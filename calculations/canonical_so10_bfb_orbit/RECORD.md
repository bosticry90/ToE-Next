# Canonical positive-Higgs benchmark: quartic orbit-space attack

1. **Scientific question:** Is the frozen positive-Higgs scalar point globally unbounded at tree level, or can its quartic be certified bounded below?
2. **Claim under test:** Find one exactly verified negative homogeneous quartic ray; otherwise establish only explicitly proved sector bounds and retain global BFB as unresolved unless a rigorous full-field certificate is obtained.
3. **Scope and authority ceiling:** This tests the single frozen point, not the canonical model class, loop stability, or phenomenology. A finite numerical search cannot certify BFB.
4. **Frozen inputs and assumptions:** Use `PARENT_ACTION_V1.md` and `canonical_so10_positive_higgs/POINT.json` without changing coefficients. Respect the real traceless 54, self-dual complex 126, complex 10, and complex singlet constraints. Optimize homogeneous quartics on normalized rays; an exact negative ray dominates lower-degree terms.
5. **Provenance and comparators:** The preceding 200-ray screen was positive but inconclusive. The local relaxed Higgs quartic is positive and does not imply global BFB.
6. **Primary method and tool:** First attack the four-invariant pure-126 orbit using exact contractions and low-support exact forms. Then search symmetry-reduced mixed-field strata with phase and ratio variation. Use exact invariant evaluation for candidate certification; numerical optimization is discovery only.
7. **Independent replay decision and reason:** Any negative ray must be recomputed from the parent monomials and replayed at higher precision or by a distinct contraction path because it would reject global stability of this point.
8. **Adversarial checks and falsifiers:** Include pure-126 generated self-dual forms, 126 plus 54/10/S, and 54+10+S strata; vary signs and phases. Check quartic homogeneity, tracelessness, and self-duality. Seek analytic or SOS bounds only where feasible.
9. **Known-limit and physical-assumption checks:** Recover the previously proved positive pure-54, pure-10, and pure-S limits. Do not infer full-orbit positivity from a restricted stratum.
10. **Stopping rule:** Stop immediately on an exactly verified negative ray. Otherwise stop after a bounded low-support orbit search and report `BFB_UNRESOLVED` unless a rigorous global lower-bound certificate is obtained. Do not start flavor or proton-decay work.
11. **Failure interpretation:** `BFB_COUNTEREXAMPLE_FOUND` rejects this parameter point, not the parent action. `BFB_UNRESOLVED` preserves only local tree-level authority. `BFB_CERTIFIED` requires a global certificate, not numerical non-detection.
12. **Result and reproducibility record:** `BFB_UNRESOLVED`. The [exact sector certificate](certify_pure_sigma.py) proves `X131 >= -2 Q1` for the entire pure self-dual-`126_H` sector by checking all 8,001 polarized basis pairs and the exchange-Casimir decomposition; its frozen coefficients give a strictly positive pure-`126_H` quartic. The `54_H+10_H` sector also has a positive exact lower quadratic form. The [mixed attack](attack_orbit.py) found no negative quartic in 11 targeted three-to-five-component strata searched with four deterministic optimizer seeds each, after direct parent-invariant interpolation checks. These slices do not certify the full orbit space. [RESULT.md](RESULT.md) records the proof, bounded search, and conditional gauge-unification handoff; no gauge calculation began.
