# Two-loop master-basis contract

This file freezes the minimum integral authority required by a future finite
`C1_GS` calculation.  It is a contract, not an implemented reducer.

At zero background momentum, the renormalized hard-region amplitude must be
reduced in dimensional regularization to a declared convention containing:

- one-loop tadpoles `A(x)` and products `A(x) A(y)`;
- the general two-loop vacuum sunset `I(x,y,z)`;
- mass derivatives/doubled-propagator descendants generated by counterterm
  insertions and tensor reduction;
- the necessary zero-mass limits with infrared poles distinguished from the
  ultraviolet poles subtracted in `MSbar`.

The evaluator must support arbitrary nondegenerate physical squared masses,
track the complete epsilon expansion needed after subdivergence subtraction,
and reproduce analytic equal-mass, one-mass-zero, and factorized limits.
Tensor numerators must be reduced before numerical evaluation.  The adopted
normalization must be explicitly mapped to the background-field amplitudes;
mixing conventions from different libraries is forbidden.

Primary technical comparators are Davydychev and Tausk's general two-loop
massive-vacuum tensor reduction ([arXiv:hep-ph/9504432](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9504432))
and the TSIL basis/evaluator of Martin and Robertson
([arXiv:hep-ph/0501132](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0501132)).  Neither is
currently integrated into this calculation, and TSIL by itself would not
supply the model-specific diagrams, counterterms, or matching subtraction.

A future master layer passes only after two independent evaluations agree on
all control limits and on a nondegenerate benchmark sample.  Until then,
`FINITE_C1_GS` remains uncomputed.
