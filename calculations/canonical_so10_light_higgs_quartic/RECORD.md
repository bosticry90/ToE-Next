# Canonical SO(10) Stage 2 light-Higgs quartic

1. **Scientific question:** Is the frozen quadratically massless SM Higgs direction locally stabilized at quartic order after heavy scalar relaxation?
2. **Claim under test:** The tree-level, canonically normalized effective coefficient of `(H†H)^2` at the exact Stage 2 point is strictly positive or strictly negative.
3. **Scope and authority ceiling:** This is a local tree-level matching calculation. It cannot establish global boundedness, loop stability, gauge unification, flavor, proton decay, or phenomenological viability.
4. **Frozen inputs and assumptions:** `PARENT_ACTION_V1.md` (SHA-256 `01323e0f6a025edd6f6669b43e7c730f5350e28d8561e72df89636862ca358ed`), `STAGE1_POINT.json`, and the algebraic tuning in `STAGE2_POINT.json`. Use the established canonical kinetic metric and exact SM embedding. The three quadratic masses are fixed by action-derived tadpoles. Goldstone directions are excluded from the inverse heavy Hessian.
5. **Provenance and comparators:** The passed full-Hessian and scalar-benchmark gates establish a locally stable Stage 1 point and a Stage 2 point with exactly one massless complex Higgs doublet, but not its quartic. The standard tree-level heavy-field elimination formula is the comparator.
6. **Primary method and tool:** Extract direct quartic and Higgs-squared/heavy cubic sources from the frozen parent monomials, form the complete heavy neutral-source Hessian, and evaluate their Schur complement with high-precision arithmetic. SymPy's exact tensor implementation is the source of the polynomial coefficients.
7. **Independent replay decision and reason:** Required because a sign error would change the benchmark's status. Replay through a separately assembled valley expansion or minimization, and vary light-field phase and heavy basis as invariance checks.
8. **Adversarial checks and falsifiers:** Verify the light quadratic eigenvalue, complete source-sector selection, Hessian positivity off symmetry orbits, cubic-source orthogonality to Goldstone directions, interpolation convergence, and stability of the quartic sign with increased precision.
9. **Known-limit and physical-assumption checks:** In the zero heavy-source limit, relaxed and fixed-heavy quartics coincide. Heavy relaxation cannot raise the quartic when the heavy Hessian is positive. One complex Higgs doublet represents four real modes; report the standard `(H†H)^2` normalization.
10. **Stopping rule:** Return `LIGHT_HIGGS_QUARTIC_POSITIVE`, `LIGHT_HIGGS_QUARTIC_NEGATIVE`, or `LIGHT_HIGGS_EFFECTIVE_POTENTIAL_BLOCKED` once the sign and its verification are settled or a necessary channel/convention remains unresolved. Do not start BFB, gauge, flavor, or proton-decay work.
11. **Failure interpretation:** A negative result rejects this particular Stage 2 tuned point as a local light-Higgs valley; it does not refute the canonical action. A blocked result means no sign claim is earned.
12. **Result and reproducibility record:** `LIGHT_HIGGS_QUARTIC_NEGATIVE`. In the standard `V_eff = lambda_eff (H†H)^2 + ...` convention, `lambda_direct = 0.199095687934763`, the heavy-relaxation subtraction is `56.6889310614242`, and `lambda_eff = -56.4898353734894` at this Stage 2 point. The direct parent-action evaluator and [compute.py](compute.py) produce the numbers; a separate polynomial expansion along the solved heavy-field valley, a heavy-coordinate shear, a Higgs-phase rotation, and two direction-rationalization precisions agree. The tensor monomial implementation is shared, so this is an independent algebraic/numerical replay, not an independently written 29-monomial tensor realization. The tuned Stage 2 point is locally unstable along its Higgs valley. Stage 1 and the canonical action retain their prior bounded statuses. See [RESULT.md](RESULT.md).
