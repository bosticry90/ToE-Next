# Positive-relaxed-quartic Higgs benchmark search

1. **Scientific question:** Does the canonical `54+126+10+S` parent action admit, near the independently stable Stage 1 vacuum, a one-light-doublet tuning whose tree-level heavy-relaxed Higgs quartic is positive?
2. **Claim under test:** Existence of one explicitly reproducible local scalar point with exactly four additional real Higgs Hessian zeros, strictly positive other physical scalar masses, and `lambda_eff >= 0.05` in the canonical `V_eff=lambda_eff(H†H)^2` normalization.
3. **Scope and authority ceiling:** A successful point would establish local tree-level scalar existence only. It would not establish boundedness of the full potential, loop-level perturbativity, gauge unification, flavor, neutrino physics, or proton-decay safety. An unsuccessful finite search is not a model no-go.
4. **Frozen inputs and assumptions:** Use `PARENT_ACTION_V1.md` (SHA-256 `01323e0f6a025edd6f6669b43e7c730f5350e28d8561e72df89636862ca358ed`), the exact Stage 1 point at `omega=sqrt(60)`, `sigma/omega=v/omega=0.1`, its action-derived tadpole masses, and its 31 unchanged Hessian-block certificate. Freeze all Stage 1 coefficients except the real parts of `z6/omega`, `zK`, `zEta`, the two pure-`10_H` quartics, and the solved `mphi2/omega²`. Imaginary parts and all other coefficients remain at Stage 1 values for this explicitly restricted search.
5. **Provenance and comparators:** The Stage 1 stable point, the negative-quartic Stage 2 point, the exact 35-block Hessian, and the parent-action monomial implementation are controls. The heavy-singlet-dominated failure motivates varying the three mixing couplings jointly; it is not a premise that the sign must improve.
6. **Primary method and tool:** Use the existing affine SM-irrep Hessian blocks to solve the smallest Higgs eigenvalue to zero in `mphi2/omega²`, rejecting candidates with other tachyons or inadequate margins. For a candidate surviving that cheap screen, compute the direct quartic and all allowed neutral heavy-source corrections from the parent action, with `lambda_eff` a simultaneous hard acceptance constraint.
7. **Independent replay decision and reason:** A positive candidate would change the canonical model's status. It requires an algebraic determinant root, direct-parent high-precision block replay, and a separately extracted polynomial along the heavy-relaxed Higgs valley; compare heavy-basis and Higgs-phase changes.
8. **Adversarial checks and falsifiers:** Test exact action tadpoles, real rank `290`/nullity `38`, one doublet zero in each conjugate block, all other physical scalar masses squared at least `0.02` in the frozen absolute units, colored squared masses at least `10^-4 omega²`, light `126_H` fraction greater than `10^-6`, positive heavy singlet/triplet blocks, Goldstone-source orthogonality, and `lambda_eff >= 0.05`. Any violation rejects that candidate.
9. **Known-limit and physical-assumption checks:** The previous Stage 2 point must reproduce its negative quartic. Pure `10_H` quartics must change the direct quartic but not the doublet Hessian or heavy-source cubic at zero `10_H` VEV. No general CP claim follows from this real-coupling slice, because the inherited `z4` remains complex.
10. **Stopping rule:** Search at most 30 determinant-tuned triples with `z6/omega,zK,zEta` in `[-0.3,0.3]` and at most 12 parent-action quartic evaluations, starting with the preregistered grid below. Pure-`10_H` quartics may be tried at `0.1`, `0.5`, or `1` independently, within the original `[-1,1]` domain. Stop at one independently verified survivor or the budget. Do not launch BFB, gauge, flavor, or proton-decay work in this calculation.
11. **Failure interpretation:** `NO_POSITIVE_QUARTIC_TUNING_IN_DECLARED_DOMAIN` requires an exhaustive certificate for the entire declared domain and will not be inferred from a finite grid. A failed finite search is `POSITIVE_QUARTIC_SEARCH_UNRESOLVED`. A positive point earns `POSITIVE_QUARTIC_HIGGS_BENCHMARK_FOUND` only at the authority ceiling above.
12. **Result and reproducibility record:** `POSITIVE_QUARTIC_HIGGS_BENCHMARK_FOUND` on the transparently amended one-dimensional path, at the local tree-level scalar authority ceiling. The frozen [POINT.json](POINT.json) has SHA-256 `476645438ea8a7ada42fea90e80174b772d45ce3e729d013d134e9848950c816`. ## Transparent post-pilot domain amendment

The first viable quadratic trial, `(0.03,0.03,0.3)`, retained the Stage 1 mixed `lambdaPhiphi1=0.19204517` and gave a raw neutral-singlet subtraction `3.25173002012406`, still far above its direct quartic. This pilot was stopped before trying the larger pure-`10_H` quartics because their maximum additive change cannot bridge a subtraction of about `52` in the standard normalization. The omitted `lambdaPhiphi1 tr(Phi²)(phi†phi)` term has a special exact property at `<phi>=0`: shifting `lambdaPhiphi1` by `delta` while shifting `mphi2/omega²` by `-delta` leaves the entire quadratic scalar Hessian, the determinant-zero light eigenvector, and all colored margins unchanged, but changes the cubic Higgs-squared source into the neutral `Phi` singlet. This is a source-level analytic search direction, not an equation-specific repair.

Before evaluating any point on that new direction, extend the search to the one-dimensional path `lambdaPhiphi1 in [-1,1]`, `mphi2/omega² = u_pilot - (lambdaPhiphi1 - 0.19204517)`, subject to the original `mphi2/omega² in [-1,1]` bound. Keep `(z6/omega,zK,zEta)=(0.03,0.03,0.3)` and the other Stage 1 coefficients fixed; allow the two pure-`10_H` quartics to move together in `{0.1,0.5,1}`. Minimize the singlet Schur correction analytically as a quadratic in `lambdaPhiphi1` using an independently evaluated source derivative. This amended path is **not** part of the original preregistered grid; report both phases separately. If it fails, the unresolved outcome applies to these explored slices only.
