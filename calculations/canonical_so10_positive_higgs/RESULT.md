The search had two clearly separated phases. In the first preregistered pilot, the quadratically viable `(z6/omega,zK,zEta)=(0.03,0.03,0.3)` point kept Stage 1 `lambdaPhiphi1=0.19204517`; its relaxed quartic was about `-51.83`. The pilot was stopped. This explicit positive witness disproves a universal sign/no-go relation that would force `lambda_eff <= 0` on the determinant-zero surface of this canonical action. It does not prove positive quartics are generic or naturally obtained.

The result is a **local tree-level scalar benchmark**, not a viable GUT. In particular, the colored floor used here is a scalar-only non-accidental-lightness test, not a proton-decay constraint. No calculation in those later domains was begun as part of this gate.
