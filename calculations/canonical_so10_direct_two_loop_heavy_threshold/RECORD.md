2. **Claim under test:** The frozen canonical action and positive-Higgs point determine a finite two-loop `Spin(10) -> SM` gauge decoupling correction in the same `MSbar` background-field convention as the passed one-loop kernel.
3. **Authority ceiling:** Two-loop heavy-boundary derivation and symbolic Yukawa dependency only. No flavor fit, proton decay, PS resummation, benchmark replacement, global BFB, or physical promotion of `g10` and `omega`.

8. **Falsifiers:** Wrong real/complex scalar counting; failure to recover `b10=-34/3`; importing a finite formula outside its assumptions; claiming scale variation bounds an arbitrary finite constant; omitting right-handed-neutrino mass dependence; or refitting with an invented threshold.
9. **Known limits:** RG invariance fixes logarithms but not the finite two-loop constants. A complete answer requires a benchmark-specific two-loop hard-region diagram/counterterm compiler, including tadpole and gauge-parameter renormalization. The physical Yukawa-dependent piece additionally requires numerical flavor data if finite right-handed-neutrino masses are retained.

11. **Negative meaning:** A blocked two-loop boundary is not evidence that the threshold is too small, that the benchmark fails, or that the canonical action fails.
12. **Result:** The RG/logarithmic and symbolic Yukawa gates advance, but the finite gauge/scalar two-loop constant is not computed. The heavy-threshold gate is `DIRECT_TWO_LOOP_HEAVY_THRESHOLD_BLOCKED`; the benchmark remains `DIRECT_GAUGE_MATCHING_UNRESOLVED`.
