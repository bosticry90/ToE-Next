# Bounded runaway/BFB probe of the positive-Higgs scalar point

1. **Scientific question:** Is the frozen positive-Higgs point's full tree-level quartic potential already falsified by a simple unbounded direction?
2. **Claim under test:** Find one explicit allowed tensor ray with a strictly negative homogeneous quartic, or report that a bounded structured probe did not settle BFB.
3. **Scope and authority ceiling:** A negative quartic ray proves the tree-level polynomial is unbounded below and rejects this point as a globally stable scalar potential. Non-detection is `BFB_UNRESOLVED`, not proof of boundedness. This gate does not assess loop effects or phenomenology.
4. **Frozen inputs and assumptions:** Use `PARENT_ACTION_V1.md` and the exact [positive-Higgs point](../canonical_so10_positive_higgs/POINT.json), SHA-256 `476645438ea8a7ada42fea90e80174b772d45ce3e729d013d134e9848950c816`. Evaluate only degree-four parent monomials on allowed canonically constrained real `54_H`, complex self-dual `126_H`, complex `10_H`, and complex singlet rays. The raw tensor coordinates are related to canonical fields by invertible fixed rescalings, which preserve the sign of a homogeneous quartic.
5. **Provenance and comparators:** The positive local Higgs quartic and full-Hessian certificate do not imply global BFB. Single-field quartics and low-support mixed rays are cheap necessary tests.
6. **Primary method and tool:** Exact parent-invariant evaluation with SymPy rational tensor rays, screened numerically and replayed as an exact algebraic expression if negative.
7. **Independent replay decision and reason:** A negative direction is consequential; verify it by separate quartic-only contraction or by exact scaling `V_4(tX)=t^4 V_4(X)` and a distinct evaluation route where practical.
8. **Adversarial checks and falsifiers:** Test pure-field rays, pairwise mixtures, and selected three-field directions, including sign/phase flips. Do not infer positivity from finitely many samples. Check that each candidate obeys tracelessness and self-duality.
9. **Known-limit and physical-assumption checks:** Positive pure-`10_H` and pure-singlet limits should reproduce their frozen positive quartics. A negative ray dominates all lower-degree terms as its norm tends to infinity, independent of the local Higgs valley.
10. **Stopping rule:** At most 200 preregistered structured rational rays; stop immediately on one exactly verified negative direction. Do not launch a global optimizer or gauge/flavor/BNV calculation in this probe.
11. **Failure interpretation:** `GLOBAL_RUNAWAY_DIRECTION_FOUND` rejects this parameter point's tree-level global scalar stability, not the canonical action. `BFB_UNRESOLVED` retains the local positive-Higgs result without claiming global stability.
12. **Result and reproducibility record:** `BFB_UNRESOLVED`. The deterministic [probe.py](probe.py) evaluated exactly 200 structured, symmetry-valid rational rays. No negative quartic was found. The smallest unnormalized tested value was `7169699367/640000000000 > 0` on the `Sigma_doublet_re` ray; this is not a global minimum or norm-comparable margin. Pure-`Phi`, pure-`10_H`, and pure-singlet analytic limits are positive, but the general mixed quartic remains uncertified. See [RESULT.md](RESULT.md).
