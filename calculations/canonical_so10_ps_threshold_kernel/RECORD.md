3. **Scope and authority ceiling:** This is a one-loop gauge-boundary calculation for this point, not a claim of global scalar stability, two-loop accuracy, fermion viability, proton decay, or viability of the whole model class. Carry `BFB_UNRESOLVED` throughout.

7. **Independent replay:** Required for PS projector ranks, scalar/vector index sums, complex-self-dual orientation, and finite vector matching, because any normalization error can supply a spurious `2--3` inverse-coupling displacement.

Stop at the first exact failure or unresolved scheme/interval map; classify the kernel separately from any later gauge solution. Do not start two-loop, flavor, or proton decay.
