2. **Claim under test:** Exact tree elimination of the specified vectors/scalars supplies a once-only Goldstone disposition and operator coefficients whose one-loop `F^2` insertions can be calculated or rigorously bounded below `0.2027005` in nonuniversal inverse-coupling differences.
3. **Scope/authority ceiling:** Tree-level upper-vector and candidate-heavy-scalar field/operator match plus only the loop insertions needed by the existing error gate. No complete upper one-loop matching, scale solve, direct fallback, flavor fit, BNV, BFB promotion, or replacement benchmark search.
