# Canonical direct physical-vacuum gauge matching

1. **Scientific question:** Does the frozen positive-Higgs canonical benchmark reproduce the three measured SM gauge couplings through one common `Spin(10)` coupling when all physical scalar and vector thresholds are matched directly at its stationary vacuum?
2. **Claim under test:** A single-scheme one-loop background-field match of all 290 positive heavy scalar directions and all 33 massive vectors can pass its internal kernel checks and either yield or exclude a common `(g10, omega)` solution with uncertainty smaller than the residual mismatch.
3. **Scope/authority ceiling:** Direct one-loop `Spin(10) -> SM` gauge-kinetic matching, the corresponding measured-coupling solve, and bounded fixed-order diagnostics only. No PS interval running, full two-loop threshold match, flavor, proton decay, benchmark replacement, or BFB promotion.
4. **Frozen inputs:** `PARENT_ACTION_V1` SHA-256 `01323e0f6a025edd6f6669b43e7c730f5350e28d8561e72df89636862ca358ed`; positive-Higgs point SHA-256 `476645438ea8a7ada42fea90e80174b772d45ce3e729d013d134e9848950c816`; physical scalar/vector ledgers; `MZ=91.1876 GeV`, `alpha_em^-1=127.93`, `sin^2 thetaW=0.23122`, `alpha_s=0.118`; GUT-normalized hypercharge; `MSbar` background-field Feynman gauge; `BFB_UNRESOLVED`.
5. **Provenance/comparators:** Complete physical SM scalar matrix log, exact physical vector Gram/index trace, the lower-vector heat-kernel calculation as a regression comparator only, and standard perturbative background-field/heat-kernel algebra rederived calculation-locally. No PS beta functions or Babu--Khan scalar thresholds enter the solve.
6. **Primary method/tool:** Exact SymPy rational heat-kernel and beta-jump checks; NumPy/SciPy physical threshold evaluation and nonlinear least-squares/root solves; independent existing tensor/matrix-log scripts for basis rotation and state-ledger replay.
7. **Independent replay decision:** Re-run scalar and vector ledger builders and both physical matrix-log checks. Reconstruct the vector finite/log ratio and direct beta jump independently inside `derive_and_solve.py`. A complete second two-loop threshold implementation is not claimed.
8. **Adversarial checks:** Double-counted Goldstones, omission of the vector finite constant, use of PS interval running, treating coarse `g_U` as input to the solve, ignoring the `g10` dependence of vector masses, solving only two couplings and calling the third a pass, or treating a gauge-only two-loop comparator as a complete uncertainty.
9. **Known limits/assumptions:** Light-field UV/EFT contributions cancel in the hard match; the tuned Higgs and PQ Goldstone remain light, and the latter is an SM singlet. Unfitted right-handed neutrinos are also SM singlets and make no one-loop gauge threshold. The measured inputs and one-loop SM running scheme are frozen rather than refitted.
10. **Stopping rule:** Return `DIRECT_GAUGE_MATCHING_PASS` only for a common perturbative solution with residual below a controlled uncertainty; `FAIL` only if the minimum residual decisively exceeds a complete admitted uncertainty; otherwise `DIRECT_GAUGE_MATCHING_UNRESOLVED`. Kernel inconsistency stops before any solve.
11. **Failure interpretation:** A failed or unresolved direct match applies first to this frozen benchmark and perturbative order. It does not reject the canonical action or the exact PS route. The direct calculation cannot establish global stability or fermion phenomenology.
12. **Result/reproducibility:** `DIRECT_GAUGE_MATCHING_UNRESOLVED`, with a separately earned `DIRECT_ONE_LOOP_KERNEL_PASS`. The best one-loop common fit has `(g10, omega)=(0.52250, 2.22e15 GeV)` only as a diagnostic and residual vector `(0.366,-1.060,0.694)`, max norm `1.060`. A gauge-only SM two-loop replay changes nonuniversal running by `0.706` and refits to max residual `0.749`; an additional threshold-order diagnostic is `0.245`. These are comparable to the mismatch, so neither pass nor fail is earned. See `RESULT.md` and `direct_matching.json`.
