## Canonical definition proposed at the charge/kinetic level

Use the simply connected gauge group `Spin(10)` (the usual particle-physics `SO(10)` shorthand) so the fermionic `16` is an actual representation; the local scalar-invariant calculation depends only on its `so(10)` Lie algebra and tensor action. A possible global quotient involving PQ is **not** fixed by this local action gate and must not be silently assumed in later axion/topology claims. Take `i,j=0,...,9`, Euclidean vector metric `delta_ij`, orientation `epsilon_0...9=+1`, and all repeated vector indices summed over **all ten values**. This is a mere 0-based relabeling of earlier 1-based exploratory scripts. `Phi_ij=Phi_ji`, `Phi_ii=0` is real (`54`); `Sigma_[ijklm]` is complex self-dual (`126`), `*Sigma=+i Sigma` in the stated orientation and `Sigma*` is the conjugate `bar126`; `phi_i` is a complex vector (`10`); `S` is a complex singlet. The kinetic convention is

```text
L_kin = (1/2) (D Phi_ij)* (D Phi_ij)
      + (1/(2 5!)) (D Sigma_ijklm)* (D Sigma_ijklm)
      + (D phi_i)* (D phi_i) + (d S)* (d S).
```

The self-dual `1/2` avoids counting both conjugate five-form sectors as independent fields. The 126 independent complementary five-index pairs each contribute a positive `|z|²` kinetic term; a sign of Hodge `*` merely swaps the labels `126`/`bar126`, but cannot be changed partway through the calculation.

Use this **explicit variant** charge assignment (integers in one normalization):

| Field | `16_F` | `Phi(54)` | `Sigma(126)` | `phi(10)` | `S` |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `q_PQ` | `+1` | `0` | `+2` | `-2` | `-4` |

Thus `16_F 16_F phi` and `16_F 16_F Sigma*` have charge zero, while `16_F 16_F phi*` is forbidden. This deliberately **does not copy** Eq. (23)'s printed `q(16_F)=-1`: with the printed scalar charges, that value makes both Eq. (55) Yukawa monomials charge `-4`. Overall reversal of *all* charges would be equivalent, but flipping only the fermion charge is a substantive reconstruction choice relative to the printed charge table. Also, Eq. (24)'s printed `eta_1 Sigma Sigma* Sigma* phi` has charge `-4` under the printed scalar assignments, although it is described as part of a PQ-invariant potential. We therefore do **not** import that tensor monomial or silently replace one conjugation. The required PQ-neutral field multiset is `Sigma Sigma Sigma* phi`. The [first gate](RESULT.md) proved it has exactly one nonzero `SO(10)` invariant; the [component gate](RESULT_V2.md) then proved its singlet-VEV insertion produces the required type of light-doublet mixing. The paper itself supplies the printed claims, not a resolution of them.

The parent action must be written as `L = L_gauge + L_kin + L_Yukawa - V`, where `V` is a **single** real polynomial. `L_Yukawa` contains only symmetric-generation `Y_10` and `Y_bar126` structures permitted above; their normalization and spinor contraction must be frozen before fitting but are not needed to search scalar extrema. `D` parity is the discrete automorphism retained by the chosen `54` vacuum and broken at the subsequent `126` step; test that assertion on generators/VEVs rather than assign it by name.
