# Canonical PS multi-spurion power-counting gate

1. **Scientific question:** Are the numerically light PS multiplets of the frozen positive-Higgs point structurally light, independently tuned, or unclassified, and does any declared counting yield a controlled upper-heavy Schur expansion?
2. **Claim under test:** Exact PS-projected intercept matrices expose the coefficient combinations behind the light sectors; a stated joint spurion family may or may not furnish a uniformly invertible heavy block and bounded mixing at `epsilon=0.1`.
3. **Scope/authority ceiling:** Quadratic hierarchy and interacting-action symmetry tests for the scalar/gauge sector, plus a provisional assumption ledger. No tree-level PS interaction matching, one-loop boundary, direct fallback admission, gauge fit, flavor or BNV calculation. Do not equate naturalness with EFT validity.
4. **Frozen inputs:** `PARENT_ACTION_V1` SHA-256 `01323e0f6a025edd6f6669b43e7c730f5350e28d8561e72df89636862ca358ed`; positive-Higgs `POINT.json` SHA-256 `476645438ea8a7ada42fea90e80174b772d45ce3e729d013d134e9848950c816`; exact PS projectors; same-coefficient stationary PS saddle with `omega_PS/omega=0.999565346637987`; canonical kinetic metric; `epsilon=0.1`. Off-shell background-path Hessians are expansion data, not pole masses.
5. **Provenance/comparators:** [multi-spurion task and fallback specification](../../docs/CANONICAL_PS_MULTISPURION_DECISION.md), [prior PS quadratic probe](../canonical_so10_ps_interval_eft/RESULT.md), independent direct-parent spot checks, and the complete 35-SM-block Hessian. Printed Babu--Khan scalar formulas are excluded.
6. **Primary method/tool:** Extract exact quadratic coefficients of each parent invariant by second directional variation on canonical PS states; express intercept matrices in the parent coefficients and evaluate at the frozen point. Use exact algebra for coefficient identities and numerical block spectra only as a comparator. Test symmetry on the interacting action, not an isolated mass matrix.
7. **Independent replay:** Essential for consequential cancellations and representation factors: compare direct parent-invariant variation against independently derived analytic formulas and the prior certified SM-block compiler.
8. **Adversarial checks:** Wrong kinetic normalization, missed holomorphic mixing, missing PS copies, accidental use of nonstationary masses, treating a tuned zero as symmetry, incomplete gauge/PQ/D or allowed-Yukawa check, singular heavy block, or a Schur remainder not bounded at `epsilon=0.1`.
9. **Known limits/assumptions:** Account for 328 real scalar directions and 21+24 gauge directions; preserve exact PS covariance at `sigma=0`; distinguish fixed-coefficient background expansion from a different-theory spurion family. Parent Yukawa contraction/normalization is not yet frozen, limiting full-theory naturalness claims.
10. **Stopping rule:** Derive suspicious-sector intercepts first. If protected/tuned classification or a uniform heavy/light bound cannot be established, return the appropriate `UNRESOLVED` or `UNPROTECTED_TUNINGS` disposition without PS interaction or loop continuation. Only a complete bound can earn `PS_MULTISPURION_EFT_CONTROLLED`.
11. **Failure interpretation:** A small unprotected mass can be a legitimate EFT input; it raises assumption cost, not an automatic EFT failure. A failed particular splitting does not refute the parent action or all EFT schemes.
12. **Result/reproducibility:** [`RESULT.md`](RESULT.md) records `PS_MULTISPURION_COUNTING_UNRESOLVED`, with `power_counting=UNRESOLVED` and `smallness_origin=MIXED`. [`extract_intercepts.py`](extract_intercepts.py) gives exact parent-coefficient PS intercept matrices and independent spectrum replay; [`analyze_cost.py`](analyze_cost.py) reports a diagnostic assumption/cancellation ledger; [`check_scalar_symmetry.py`](check_scalar_symmetry.py) certifies that only PQ remains among connected fieldwise scalar rephasings relevant to the claimed light combinations. [`check_candidate_split.py`](check_candidate_split.py) distinguishes the fixed-coefficient off-shell path. [`test_joint_family.py`](test_joint_family.py) checks both frozen hashes, constructs one explicit different-theory family passing through the physical point, verifies its tadpoles, and evaluates provisional scalar heavy/mixing/Schur bounds. The family provides partial numerical quadratic control but not a certified covariant PS field/operator split or a whole-retained-sector EFT error bound. The direct fallback and all loop continuations remain unadmitted.
