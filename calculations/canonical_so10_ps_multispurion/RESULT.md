## Declared joint family and bounded diagnostic

[`test_joint_family.py`](test_joint_family.py) defines a **new formal family of scalar actions**, not a variation of the frozen physical theory. Let `x=sigma/omega=v/omega` range over `[0,0.1]`, fix `omega=sqrt(60)`, and multiply `muPhi,lambdaPhi1,lambdaPhi2,mphi2,muPhiPhi,lambdaPhiphi1,lambdaPhiphi2` by `(x/0.1)^2`, while multiplying `z6,zK` by `x/0.1`. All other free coefficients remain fixed. Solve `mPhi2,mSigma2,mS2` from the three *parent-action* tadpoles at every `x`. It agrees with the exact frozen positive-Higgs point at `x=0.1` and has vanishing tadpole residuals throughout. At `x=0`, the provisional low scalar block has zero intercept, while the candidate `126_H` `(6,1,1)+(15,2,2)` upper block has minimum eigenvalue `0.286999 omega²`. This explicit counting pays for light `10_H/54_H` sectors by varying multiple otherwise independent parent coefficients; it is not claimed to be symmetry-protected or uniquely required.

On this family, the previously certified affine SM blocks are polynomials in `x` of degree at most four. Reconstructing their coefficients from five nodes and replaying at an independent sixth point gives maximum matrix-entry disagreement `5.6e-17` in `m²/omega²`. For the *candidate 132-real upper-heavy scalar subspace only*, a conservative operator-norm polynomial triangle estimate gives, on `0<=x<=0.1`,

```text
lambda_min(H_HH) >= 0.077574 omega²,
||H_LH|| <= 0.032270 omega²,
||H_LH H_HH^-1 H_HL|| <= 0.013425 omega².
```

These are numerically conservative floating-point estimates, **not** directed-interval or exact-rational certificates. At the physical endpoint, direct block evaluation gives `lambda_min(H_HH)=0.286714 omega²`, `||H_LH||=0.031450 omega²`, and maximum blockwise Schur norm `0.003444 omega²`. However the largest provisionally retained scalar block has norm `0.201845 omega²` there, approximately `0.70` of the candidate heavy minimum. The endpoint therefore does not display an obviously clean *whole-retained-sector* mass hierarchy. The provisional low side also includes the 24 first-stage gauge-orbit directions; removing these and matching the massive vectors covariantly has **not** been done. This candidate gives partial quadratic control, not `PS_INTERVAL_EFT_DEFINED` and not even a certified full scalar/vector truncation error at the requested accuracy.

A separate [same-coefficient off-shell background diagnostic](check_candidate_split.py) keeps all parent coefficients fixed, follows the nearby `Phi` tadpole root, and obtains a stronger sign-aware numerical heavy lower estimate `0.253952 omega²` and Schur upper estimate `0.004101 omega²`. The specific all-`10_H/54_H`-retained counting above uses unprotected-looking small scalar inputs and a numerically weak retained/heavy mass gap at `epsilon=0.1`; it is not automatically the only admissible assignment. The next scientific decision must freeze an accuracy target and a covariant disposition for the upper Goldstones/vectors and borderline scalar blocks, then strengthen the numerical bounds to certified ones if the PS interval is pursued.
