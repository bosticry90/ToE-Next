No one-loop boundary pass, gauge-scale fit, BFB certificate, flavor, or proton decay. A formal `sigma=0` PS phase need not be a second physical vacuum if a controlled expansion from the actual stationary point exists.

8. **Adversarial checks and falsifiers:** Unsuppressed `O(omega²)` mass in a proposed retained multiplet; singular heavy inverse; uncontrolled heavy/light mixing; noncovariant field split; missing or double-counted states; a discarded induced operator capable of an unsuppressed lower one-loop `F²` insertion.

If hierarchy fails, stop with `PS_INTERVAL_EFT_POWER_COUNTING_FAIL`; if classification or scaling remains unearned, `PS_INTERVAL_EFT_BLOCKED`. Only a complete interaction/operator and lower-`F²` relevance ledger earns `PS_INTERVAL_EFT_DEFINED`.
11. **Failure interpretation:** Failure applies to this benchmark and declared two-scale truncation, not automatically to the parent action or all possible interval schemes. Lack of a stationary `sigma=0` vacuum alone is not failure.
