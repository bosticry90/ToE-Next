# Canonical direct gauge matching: next-order running gate

1. **Scientific question:** Does replacing the gauge-only comparator by a perturbatively consistent coupled two-loop SM evolution turn the frozen benchmark's direct one-loop threshold match into a controlled gauge-unification pass or fail?
2. **Claim under test:** The passed direct one-loop heavy kernel, combined with two-loop SM gauge running and the required Yukawa/Higgs evolution, either admits one common `(g10, omega)` solution or leaves a mismatch that can be compared with a closed higher-order matching uncertainty.
3. **Scope/authority ceiling:** Two-loop SM gauge running, one-loop third-family Yukawa/Higgs running required at that gauge order, the already-passed one-loop physical heavy threshold, matching-scale variation, and an explicit matching-order audit. No two-loop heavy decoupling is silently inferred; no flavor fit, proton decay, PS resummation, benchmark replacement, or BFB promotion.
4. **Frozen inputs:** `PARENT_ACTION_V1` SHA-256 `01323e0f6a025edd6f6669b43e7c730f5350e28d8561e72df89636862ca358ed`; positive-Higgs point SHA-256 `476645438ea8a7ada42fea90e80174b772d45ce3e729d013d134e9848950c816`; passed `DIRECT_ONE_LOOP_KERNEL`; the same measured gauge inputs as the preceding calculation; `MSbar`; GUT-normalized `g1`; reference weak boundary at `mt=173.34 GeV` with `(yt,yb,ytau,lambda)=(0.93714,0.0164,0.0102,0.12604)`.
5. **Provenance/comparators:** Luo--Xiao, *Two-loop Renormalization Group Equations in the Standard Model*, [arXiv:hep-ph/0207271](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0207271), for the SM RGE convention; Buttazzo et al., *Investigating the near-criticality of the Higgs boson*, [arXiv:1307.3536](https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.3536), for the reference `MSbar` weak-scale top/Higgs boundary. The prior gauge-only two-loop result is a regression comparator.
6. **Primary method/tool:** SciPy adaptive integration of the coupled seven-variable `(g1,g2,g3,yt,yb,ytau,lambda)` system; nonlinear least-squares and pairwise roots for the direct matching equations; exact reuse only of the frozen one-loop physical mass thresholds.
7. **Independent replay decision:** Recompute the flow in inverse-coupling variables `Ai=alpha_i^-1` with a separately written beta function and the `DOP853` integrator, then replay the central fit. Agreement is required at `2e-8` in the inverse-coupling residuals.
8. **Adversarial checks:** Broad weak-boundary variations; matching-scale scan `mu/omega in {0.5,0.75,1,1.5,2}`; pairwise exact-channel solves; preservation of the passed one-loop field ledger; no promotion of the small matching-scale envelope into a rigorous two-loop threshold bound.
9. **Known limits/assumptions:** One-loop Yukawa running is sufficient inside a two-loop gauge beta function. The Higgs quartic is evolved but does not enter the gauge beta function at two loops. Lighter Yukawas are trace-suppressed. A complete fixed-order uncertainty requires model-dependent two-loop heavy decoupling; its Yukawa-dependent part is underdefined because the canonical Yukawa matrices are not frozen.
10. **Stopping rule:** `PASS` requires a common perturbative solution within a controlled complete uncertainty; `FAIL` requires the minimum residual to decisively exceed that uncertainty; otherwise retain `DIRECT_GAUGE_MATCHING_UNRESOLVED`.
11. **Failure interpretation:** An unresolved result means the known next-order SM running is insufficient to adjudicate the benchmark while the two-loop heavy boundary remains uncomputed. It does not invalidate the one-loop kernel, canonical action, or scalar benchmark.
12. **Result/reproducibility:** `DIRECT_SM_TWO_LOOP_RUNNING_PASS` and `DIRECT_GAUGE_MATCHING_UNRESOLVED`. The known next-order central refit has residual `(0.260915,-0.745476,0.484795)` and no common root. Matching-scale variation changes the maximum residual only across `0.74172--0.74931`, and broad weak-boundary variations by less than `5.8e-5`; however, no rigorous two-loop heavy-threshold bound is available. Run `run_next_order.py` and `independent_replay.py`; see `RESULT.md` and `next_order.json`.
